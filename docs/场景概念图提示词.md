## ruins

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: environment concept art for a small playable turn-based fantasy RPG called Grande, to be manually rebuilt in Blender. Create ONE landscape game-environment concept illustration, orthographic/isometric three-quarter view from the front-right, clearly readable chunky yet refined mid-poly geometry, bevels and restrained material variation, simple inexpensive modular production design. A clear central combat floor roughly 12 by 12 metres occupies the foreground/middle with unobstructed sightlines and visible flat floor; 1 main architectural landmark at the back, 2-3 reusable prop types, sparse outlying structures. No characters, no monsters, no interface, no labels or text. Single coherent environment, not a sheet of several views. Beauty through composition and palette, not thousands of tiny props. Soft atmospheric depth, readable warm/cool lighting, plausible geometry. NOT a tiny round arena floating in empty blue void. The foreground perimeter must remain low to preserve combat visibility. Subject: abandoned crystal-powered city gate. An asymmetrical rectangular courtyard paved with large chipped warm gray sandstone slabs, two broken stair approaches, a tall half-collapsed arched gate and one fluted tower behind it, three fractured pillars, a few blue-green crystal clusters integrated into broken masonry, moss pockets and angular cliff foundations. Warm late afternoon key light, desaturated teal shadow, cream worn stone, copper weathering. Calm ancient ruin. A few large silhouette forms only; gate clearly the main landmark.
