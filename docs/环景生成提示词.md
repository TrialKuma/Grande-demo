# 3.9 五处远景

使用内置 ImageGen，以已有场景概念图为视觉参考，分别生成新的 2:1 远景。它们作为可旋转的环境背景，配合近场地形、水面和雾使用；不是把概念图自动重建为三维场景。

## ruins

成品：[ruins.png](../public/backdrops/ruins.png)

Create a finished environment background texture for the Grande fantasy RPG, based on the color palette and distant world in the reference concept. This is a NEW 360-degree equirectangular panoramic skybox image, 2:1 aspect ratio, ideally 4096x2048. Horizon exactly halfway vertically; longitude full 360 degrees; seamless left/right edge with matching lighting and landscape; upper half mostly sky, lower half distant landscape and water or terrain fading toward nadir. Painterly stylized game art with simple readable masses and atmospheric depth, restrained detail to complement low-poly 3D foreground. NO foreground battle arena, NO isolated floating platform, NO characters, NO interface, NO letters, NO frames. View from eye level above the terrain rather than an isometric concept layout. Keep strong landmarks on or below horizon, leave sky calm.
Theme: Warm late afternoon; a broad connected sandstone ruin valley with crumbling aqueducts, old citadel towers and cypress on cliff terraces, hazy blue lake and mountain ranges. Warm amber sunlight left, muted teal distance. All ruins rooted in land.
